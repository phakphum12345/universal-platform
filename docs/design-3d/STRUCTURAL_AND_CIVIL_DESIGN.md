# Structural and Civil Design Intelligence

**Status:** PROPOSED capability specification

## 1. Scope

Support conceptual and analytical design of structures, buildings, frames, supports, foundations and infrastructure components. This is an engineering-assistance specification, not a professional design certification.

## 2. Capability areas

Geometry and spatial layout; load cases; structural systems; material properties; member sizing; stress and deflection; stability and buckling; vibration; connections; foundation/soil inputs; environmental loads; durability; constructability; and quantity estimation.

## 3. Standards and jurisdiction

Applicable building codes, design standards, local regulations, load combinations and professional sign-off requirements must be selected for the jurisdiction and project. If the governing standard is unknown, mark the design basis OPEN and do not claim code compliance.

## 4. Data requirements

Site and environmental conditions; occupancy/use; geometry; material grade; load sources; soil/geotechnical data; exposure and durability; construction methods; and project constraints. Each input needs provenance and confidence.

## 5. Verification

Independent calculations; load-path review; stability checks; serviceability; connection review; sensitivity; constructability; and qualified professional review. Model results must not replace site investigation or required inspection.

## 6. Safety boundary

No construction authorization, structural certification or permit claim. High-consequence structural outputs require qualified local professionals and regulatory review.

## 7. Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Design basis and jurisdiction are explicit.
- [ ] Loads and combinations cite their sources.
- [ ] Material and soil inputs have provenance.
- [ ] Structural assumptions and limitations are visible.
- [ ] Independent review is required before real-world use.
